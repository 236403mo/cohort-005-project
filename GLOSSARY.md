# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language for giving a learner their money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have the money for one Purchase returned, with their reason in their own words. It is Pending, Approved, or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, cancel request

**Pending**:
The state of a Refund Request that no Admin has decided yet. A Purchase has at most one Pending Refund Request at a time. A learner cannot withdraw one.
_Avoid_: Requested, open, waiting, raised

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request an Admin has agreed to. Approval produces a Refund.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request an Admin has turned down. No money is returned, and the learner keeps the course. If the Purchase still has Refund Eligibility, the learner may make a new Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund**:
Money we send back to a learner because their Refund Request was Approved. A Refund exists only once a request is Approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, cancellation

**Refund Amount**:
The money a Refund returns: always the full amount the learner actually paid for the Purchase, never the course's list price. There are no partial Refunds.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price, partial refund

**Refunded**:
The state of a Purchase whose Refund Request was Approved. The learner's access to the course ends.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, reversed

### Refund Eligibility

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a learner may make a Refund Request. It counts whole days, so the fourteenth day after the purchase is included in full.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request made against it. It is eligible when it is inside the Refund Window, the learner has watched under 2 hours and completed under half the course's lessons, it has no Pending Refund Request, and it is not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancellable

### Not a Refund

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, which pulls the money back without our agreement. It is never a Refund Request or a Refund, and the two words must never be swapped.
